## ⚠ Loader corrections

The concept's "performance trifecta" names **Sodium + Lithium** (Fabric). On Forge 1.20.1 use **Embeddium** (Sodium) and **Canary** (Lithium), plus **FerriteCore**. Add **Oculus** if shaders are wanted.

## Notes

- **Citadel** required for Ice and Fire (and Alex's mods if added).
- Ad Astra in era IV pairs well with Mekanism power; ensure oxygen/space configs are tuned in `overrides/config/`.
